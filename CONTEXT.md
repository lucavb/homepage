# Luca Becker homepage

Personal site and technical blog. One context: how the site talks about its blog content, its pages, and its appearance.

## Language

**Published post**:
A blog entry that is not a draft. Lists, feeds, and relatedness queries all mean the same thing by it, and all order published posts newest-first.
_Avoid_: live post, visible post

**Draft preview**:
A draft blog entry rendered at its own URL for review, while staying excluded from published lists and search.
_Avoid_: unpublished post

**Related posts**:
The three-tier rule for what to show after a blog entry: curated picks first, then tag overlap, then recency. Capped at three.
_Avoid_: similar posts, recommended posts

**Hero image**:
The image that represents a blog entry in previews and social cards: the entry's thumbnail if set, else its hero image file, else the site default.
_Avoid_: cover image, og image (that is the page-level artifact, not the per-entry source)

**Page meta**:
The rules every page must satisfy to be shareable: title suffixing, canonical URL, social preview image, structured data.
_Avoid_: SEO tags, metadata (too generic)

**Theme mode**:
The site appearance preference — light, dark, or following the OS setting — remembered per visitor.
_Avoid_: dark mode (incomplete: mode also covers light and system), color scheme
